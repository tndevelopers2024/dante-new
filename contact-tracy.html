Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful details about visiting Dante Gonzales Orthodontics in Tracy, CA.

How do I get to the Tracy orthodontic office?

Our office is located at 1417 N Tracy Blvd, Tracy, CA 95376. We are easily accessible from I-205 and offer dedicated, free parking directly in front of the clinic.

Are consultations complimentary in Tracy?

Yes! Your initial smile assessment with Dr. Dante Gonzales is 100% complimentary. It includes digital 3D scans, diagnostic photos, and a custom treatment plan with transparent financial options.

What treatments are available at the Tracy location?

Our Tracy office offers full orthodontic services including traditional metal braces, clear ceramic braces, Invisalign for adults, Invisalign Teen, accelerated orthodontics, and sleep apnea oral appliance treatments.

Do you accept orthodontic insurance in Tracy?

Yes, we accept and maximize most dental and orthodontic insurance plans. Our team will verify your benefits before treatment and provide 0% interest flexible in-house payment financing options.
