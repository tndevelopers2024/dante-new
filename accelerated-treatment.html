Get Straight Teeth Up To 50% Faster With Propel At Dante Gonzales Orthodontics

Propel

Propel Treatment provides innovative devices and procedures to shift teeth much quicker than ordinary treatment. Dr. Gonzales can use Propel in combination with current orthodontic treatments, including Invisalign and traditional metal braces, to help move teeth into their desired position up to 50% faster than teeth can move with Invisalign and traditional metal braces alone.
