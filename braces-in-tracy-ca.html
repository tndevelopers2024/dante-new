Keeping Oral Health Strong During Treatment

Whether you wear braces or clear aligners, keeping up with your oral health is essential. We guide patients on brushing techniques, using interdental brushes, and cleaning trays properly. Neglecting cleaning can cause plaque or discomfort, so we check this during every visit.

Our orthodontist and team make sure your oral health stays balanced throughout. We even show kids short demos on how to clean braces properly. Feels like teaching them something useful for life.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What types of braces do you offer in Tracy CA?

We provide traditional braces, ceramic options, and clear aligners like Invisalign, each customised for comfort, results, and your unique orthodontic treatment goals.

2. Do you accept dental insurance for braces?

Yes, our office accepts most dental insurance plans. We also offer flexible payment plans and free orthodontic consultation for new patients.

3. How long does braces treatment usually take?

Treatment time depends on each case, typically between 12–24 months. Regular visits and proper care help achieve a straight, healthy, and confident smile.

4. Can adults get braces or Invisalign treatment at your Tracy CA office?

Absolutely. Many of our patients are adults who choose to improve their smiles later in life. Whether you prefer traditional braces or clear aligners like Invisalign, Dr. Gonzales will create a personalized treatment plan that fits your goals, schedule, and lifestyle.

5. What happens after my braces or Invisalign treatment is complete?

Once your treatment ends, we provide custom retainers to help maintain your new smile. Our team will also schedule follow-up visits to make sure your teeth stay beautifully aligned. We’re here for the long run—keeping your smile looking its best for years to come.
