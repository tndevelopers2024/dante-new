A letter from our founder

Dear Friend,

When I talk about braces, not only do I speak from my years of experience, extensive training, and education, but also with the understanding that I gained from my own time spent in braces. I know firsthand how it feels to wear braces, because I wore them when I was younger. Seeing the confidence in someone’s new smile reminds me of how I felt after I was done with my orthodontic treatment and it’s incredibly rewarding.

As the sole orthodontist and dentist on the advisory board of Sharecare.com, a company founded by Dr. Oz and Jeff Arnold (founder of WebMD), I get to help thousands of people get their dental and orthodontic questions answered with accurate information. As a board-certified orthodontist, I have had professional athletes put their trust in me to bring out their greatest smiles. Since 1998, I have transformed over 12,000 smiles, and I can’t wait to show you what a beautiful smile can do for you.

Orthodontics is our expertise, but customer service and helping people is our passion.

With same-day appointments available and the option to receive same-day braces after your initial consultation, you can be on your way to a stunning smile sooner than you think. Every time one of our patients walks out of our office for the last time, they leave with confidence because their smile is backed by the Gonzales Lifetime Guarantee. Not only are we going to transform your smile, but we can get you out of braces faster than ever with our accelerated treatments. We can’t wait to meet you!

Come experience the difference at Dante Gonzales Orthodontics. We use only top of the line braces and Invisalign along with the best customer service possible. We serve patients in and around Dublin, Pleasanton, San Ramon, Danville, Livermore, Castro Valley, Tracy, San Leandro, Hayward and Fremont, California (CA), Manteca, Stockton and Lathrop.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about braces, Invisalign, or visiting Dante Gonzales Orthodontics? Here are answers to questions our Dublin and Tracy patients ask most.

How much does orthodontic treatment or Invisalign cost?

At Dante Gonzales Orthodontics, the cost of Invisalign is comparable to traditional braces. We believe financial considerations shouldn’t stand between you and your ideal smile. We offer 0% interest flexible in-house payment options, maximize your dental insurance benefits, and accept HSA/FSA funds. Your initial consultation and 3D smile scan are 100% complimentary with no obligation.

How do I schedule my complimentary consultation?

You can schedule easily using our online appointment form, taking our 30-second Invisalign Quiz, or calling our Dublin office at 925-828-2244 or Tracy office at 209-835-0977. Same-day consultations are often available!

Do I need a referral from my dentist to visit?

No referral is required! While many Bay Area general dentists refer their patients directly to Dr. Dante Gonzales, anyone can contact us directly to schedule a consultation for themselves or their child.

What is the best age to begin orthodontic treatment?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation around age 7. At this stage, Dr. Gonzales can evaluate jaw development and emerging adult teeth. However, healthy teeth can be moved at any age — today, over 30% of our orthodontic patients are adults!

Can Invisalign fix complex crowding, spacing, or bite problems?

Yes! As a Diamond Invisalign Provider ranked in the top 1% across North America, Dr. Dante Gonzales routinely treats moderate-to-severe crowding, gaps, overbites, underbites, and crossbites with clear aligners that other providers might only treat with metal braces.

What are your office hours and do you offer Saturday appointments?

We provide extended hours to fit busy family schedules, opening as early as 7:30 am on Tuesdays and Wednesdays and staying open until 6:00 pm on Mondays and Thursdays. Saturday morning appointments (8:15 am – 1:30 pm) are also available by appointment.

What is the Gonzales Lifetime Guarantee?

We stand behind your smile for life! If your teeth ever shift in the future due to retainer non-wear or natural age-related movement, Dr. Gonzales will place you back into treatment with no new setup fees.
