Our story

Where careful orthodontics meets genuine hospitality

Dante Gonzales Orthodontics opened in Dublin in September 1998. Dr. Gonzales had spent a year working alongside several Bay Area orthodontists, taking note of what made a practice excellent — clinically and for the people sitting in the chair — and built his own around those lessons.

Today, from our offices in Dublin and Tracy, we treat children, teens and adults with metal braces, clear braces, Invisalign, Invisalign Teen and surgical orthodontics, plus accelerated options that can shorten treatment by up to 50%. Every finished case is backed by the Gonzales Lifetime Guarantee.

Orthodontics is our expertise, but customer service and helping people is our passion.
