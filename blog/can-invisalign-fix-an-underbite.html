Can Invisalign Fix an Underbite? What Orthodontists Want You to Know

Dante Gonzales Orthodontics June 1, 2026 11 min read

On this page

An underbite is more than a cosmetic concern. When the lower teeth protrude beyond the upper front teeth, it creates a misaligned bite that places uneven stress on the jaw joints, accelerates enamel wear, and makes basic tasks like chewing and speaking noticeably harder. Many patients arrive at Dante Gonzales Orthodontics asking whether Invisalign clear aligners can correct this kind of bite problem or whether they are limited to straightening teeth alone. The answer depends on the type and severity of the underbite and for most patients, it is far more encouraging than they expect.

What Is an Underbite?

An underbite, also known as a Class III malocclusion, occurs when the lower teeth extend or protrude beyond the upper front teeth when the mouth is closed. This misaligned bite affects both the appearance and oral function of the patient. The condition ranges from barely noticeable where only a few lower teeth edge past the uppers to more pronounced cases where the entire lower jaw visibly extends forward, sometimes contributing to a more prominent chin.

Common Causes of an Underbite

Underbites develop for several reasons. In many cases, the cause is genetic, as jaw size, bone structure, and tooth position are largely inherited traits. Childhood habits are another significant factor prolonged thumb sucking, extended pacifier use, mouth breathing, and other bad oral habits can gradually shift the jaw and teeth out of proper alignment during development. Regardless of origin, an untreated underbite creates a cascade of oral health problems over time, including:

  • Accelerated wear on the enamel of both upper and lower teeth
  • Chronic jaw pain and temporomandibular joint (TMJ) discomfort
  • Increased risk of tooth wear and chipping
  • Difficulty biting into or chewing food properly
  • Speech issues, including lisping or altered pronunciation
  • Bite issues that worsen if left untreated

Can Invisalign Fix an Underbite?

Yes, Invisalign can fix an underbite effectively in many patients, particularly those with mild to moderate underbites rooted in dental misalignment rather than severe skeletal jaw discrepancy. Invisalign clear aligners work by gradually shifting misaligned teeth into proper alignment, correcting the bite relationship without the need for metal braces or wires.

The key distinction orthodontists at Dante Gonzales Orthodontics evaluate is whether the underbite is dental or skeletal. A dental underbite is caused primarily by tooth position the teeth themselves are tilted or placed incorrectly. A skeletal underbite involves the bone structure itself, where the lower jaw has physically grown too far forward or the upper jaw is structurally underdeveloped. Invisalign fix results are most predictable and complete in dental underbite cases and mild to moderate underbites with a strong skeletal component in growing patients.

Mild Underbites

Mild underbites are highly responsive to Invisalign treatment. In these cases, the aligners reposition the upper teeth forward and guide the lower teeth back into proper alignment, achieving a healthier bite with minimal additional orthodontic intervention. Mild underbites may be corrected in approximately 10-12 months depending on patient compliance and case complexity.

Moderate Underbites

Moderate underbites require a more detailed customized treatment plan. Dante Gonzales Orthodontics incorporates attachments small tooth-colored anchors bonded to the teeth to enhance aligner grip and facilitate precise movement of the jaws into alignment. Elastics worn between the upper and lower arches apply additional corrective force. Treatment duration for moderate underbites typically ranges from 12 to 18 months.

Severe Underbites

For severe underbites where the misalignment is due to significant skeletal issues, Invisalign alone may not be sufficient and could require additional orthodontic treatments or surgical intervention. In these cases, Dante Gonzales Orthodontics coordinates a combined approach using Invisalign before and after jaw surgery to achieve full correction. Severe underbites that require surgery fall outside what clear aligners alone can address, and candidacy is determined through an initial consultation and 3D imaging assessment.

How Invisalign Treatment Corrects an Underbite

Invisalign treatment is customized using 3D imaging technology, ensuring that each set of aligners is tailored to fit the patient's teeth perfectly and gradually move them into the correct position. Initial evaluations include 3D digital scans to create a virtual roadmap of tooth movement before a single aligner is fabricated. Here is how the process works for underbite correction:

Precision Tooth Movement

Each aligner in the series exerts controlled pressure to retract the lower teeth and advance the upper teeth forward simultaneously. This staged repositioning changes how the upper and lower arches relate to each other, correcting the bite relationship over the full course of treatment. On average, patients can start noticing changes in their teeth alignment within the first few weeks of wearing Invisalign aligners, though the overall treatment duration depends on individual compliance and case complexity.

Attachments and Elastics

Attachments may be necessary to enhance aligner grip and facilitate movement of the teeth into alignment for underbite cases. These tooth-colored buttons are bonded to specific teeth and allow the aligners to apply more precise directional force. Elastics work alongside the aligners to guide teeth into position, especially in moderate underbite cases. Elastics always play a key role when correcting underbites.

Invisalign Mandibular Advancement for Younger Patients

For younger patients who are still growing, Invisalign offers a specialized mandibular advancement feature with precision wings built directly into the aligner trays. This holds the lower jaw in a slightly corrected position during wear, helping guide jaw development before the underbite becomes a more complex structural problem. This approach reduces the likelihood of requiring surgical intervention later in life.

Compliance and Wear Time

The success of Invisalign treatment for underbites largely depends on the severity of the underbite and the patient's compliance with wearing the aligners for 20 to 22 hours per day. Unlike traditional braces, the removability of Invisalign aligners means results are directly tied to consistent wear. Check-ups during Invisalign treatment are typically scheduled every 10-14 weeks, reducing office visits compared to traditional braces while keeping the treatment plan on track.

Invisalign for Underbite vs. Traditional Metal Braces

Both Invisalign and traditional braces are effective tools for correcting underbites. The right treatment depends on the severity of the case, the patient's age, and their overall oral health needs. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign clear aligners are virtually invisible, making them a discreet and comfortable alternative for adults and teens who may be self-conscious about wearing metal brackets.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Invisalign Underbite Treatment?

Not every underbite is the same, and the right treatment is determined through a thorough initial consultation and clinical evaluation at Dante Gonzales Orthodontics. Patients who respond best to Invisalign for underbite correction typically include:

  • Adults and teens with mild to moderate underbites caused primarily by tooth misalignment
  • Patients whose dental problems involve tooth position rather than extreme skeletal jaw discrepancy
  • Growing adolescents where early intervention can guide jaw and bone structure development
  • Patients who prioritize a virtually invisible, discreet and comfortable alternative to metal braces
  • Patients with overall oral health needs that align with removable appliance therapy

Patients with severe underbites linked to significant skeletal jaw issues may need alternative treatments or a combined approach. A skilled dentist or orthodontist will evaluate whether Invisalign, traditional braces, or a surgical plan best addresses the individual's specific bite issues and oral health needs.

The Oral Health Impact of an Untreated Underbite

An underbite does not improve on its own. Left untreated, underbites can lead to complications such as difficulty in biting and chewing, speech issues, increased wear on tooth enamel, and jaw pain due to strain on the temporomandibular joint (TMJ). The ongoing misaligned bite also creates areas that are difficult to clean properly, elevating the risk of tooth decay, gum disease, and other oral health problems over time.

Beyond functional concerns, an untreated underbite can contribute to facial asymmetry and a more prominent chin as the jaw continues to develop in its misaligned position. Addressing the underbite early while bone structure is still responsive to orthodontic guidance protects both the teeth and the overall oral health of the patient for the long term.

Why Early Underbite Treatment Produces Better Results

The duration of Invisalign treatment for an underbite varies depending on the severity of the condition, with treatment typically lasting between 12 to 24 months. Starting treatment earlier, particularly during adolescence (ages 8-12), shortens this timeline and reduces the complexity of correction because the bone structure and jaw are still growing and more responsive to orthopedic movement.

Early intervention with Invisalign for underbite correction in younger patients often eliminates the need for surgical intervention that would otherwise become necessary in adulthood. Correcting crowded teeth and misaligned bite patterns while the jaw is still developing preserves tooth structure, improves bite function, and sets the foundation for lasting overall oral health.

Determining whether Invisalign can fix your specific underbite requires a thorough clinical evaluation not a generalized answer. At Dante Gonzales Orthodontics, every underbite correction plan is built on advanced 3D imaging, comprehensive bite analysis, and a deep understanding of both dental and skeletal underbite mechanics. Whether you need a straightforward Invisalign treatment plan or a combination approach, the right treatment is the one designed around your unique anatomy.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can Invisalign Fix an Underbite Without Surgery?

Invisalign can effectively fix mild to moderate dental underbites without surgery by gradually shifting misaligned teeth into proper alignment. For severe underbites where the misalignment is caused by significant skeletal jaw issues, Invisalign alone may not be sufficient, and surgical intervention may be required alongside orthodontic treatment. An initial consultation with Dante Gonzales Orthodontics will determine which approach is right for your case.

2. What Is an Underbite and How Does It Affect Oral Health?

An underbite, also known as a Class III malocclusion, occurs when the lower teeth protrude beyond the upper front teeth, affecting both bite function and appearance. Left untreated, underbites lead to oral health problems including tooth decay, gum disease, jaw pain, enamel wear, and speech issues. Correcting the underbite with Invisalign treatment restores proper alignment and protects long-term oral health.

3. How Long Does Invisalign Take to Correct an Underbite?

The duration of Invisalign treatment for underbites varies depending on severity. Mild underbites may be corrected in approximately 10-12 months, while moderate to severe cases typically require 12 to 24 months of treatment. Patient compliance wearing aligners, and elastics, for 20 to 22 hours per day is the single biggest factor that determines how closely treatment stays on schedule.

4. Is Invisalign for Underbite Suitable for Teenagers?

Yes. Invisalign offers mandibular advancement features specifically designed for younger patients, built directly into the aligner trays to help guide jaw development during growth. Treating an underbite during adolescence, when bone structure is still responsive, typically produces faster results with less complexity and significantly reduces the likelihood of requiring surgical intervention later in adulthood.

5. How Is Invisalign Different from Traditional Braces for Underbite Correction?

Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign clear aligners are virtually invisible, removable for eating and cleaning, and made of smooth flexible plastic that causes minimal soft tissue irritation. For mild to moderate underbites, Invisalign delivers clinically comparable results to traditional braces while offering a discreet and comfortable alternative. Check-ups are scheduled every 10-12 weeks, fewer than the typical braces schedule, giving patients more flexibility throughout treatment.
