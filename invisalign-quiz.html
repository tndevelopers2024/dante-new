    Frequently Asked Questions

    Invisalign Clear Aligners FAQ

    Have questions about clear aligners? Here are answers to common questions from our patients in Dublin and Tracy.

    Can Invisalign fix severe crowding or bite issues?

    Yes! As a Diamond Invisalign Provider in the top 1% nationwide, Dr. Dante Gonzales utilizes advanced attachments, elastics, and precision digital staging to correct severe crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, and crossbites that other offices might deem unsuitable for aligners.

    How much does Invisalign cost compared to metal braces?

    At Dante Gonzales Orthodontics, the cost of Invisalign is comparable to traditional braces. We believe financial considerations shouldn’t prevent you from choosing the most comfortable treatment. We offer flexible in-house payment options with 0% interest, maximize your dental insurance benefits, and accept HSA/FSA funds.

    How many hours a day do I need to wear my aligners?

    You should wear your Invisalign aligners for 20 to 22 hours per day, removing them only to eat, drink anything other than cool water, and brush and floss your teeth. Because they are removable, there are zero dietary restrictions!

    What happens during my complimentary smile consultation?

    Your visit includes an in-depth clinical examination by Dr. Gonzales, digital photographs, and a fast 3D iTero scan with an instant outcome simulation so you can see your straight smile before you commit. Our treatment coordinator will also outline your exact treatment timeline and transparent pricing.
