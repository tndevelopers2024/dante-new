Three ways to straighten

Metal Braces, Clear Braces & Clarity Braces In Dublin and Tracy, CA

Every bracket we bond is chosen for the case in front of us. Here is how the three systems differ — and who each one suits best.

  • Most versatile

    Metal Braces

    Today’s braces in Dublin and Tracy, CA, are smaller and sleeker than ever before! We have used traditional metal braces for years because of the fantastic results we can achieve with them. They are still the standard when it comes to getting you your best smile possible! Some cases may need to rely on the tried-and-true traditional metal braces for optimal results. You have the opportunity to pick the colors of your elastics whenever you have them replaced!

  • Discreet

    Ceramic Braces

    We believe every individual has the right to live their life with a smile they truly love. Ceramic braces in Dublin and Tracy, CA, help you achieve the smile of your dreams more discreetly that you ever imagined. Adults and some teens choose ceramic braces because they blend in with their teeth and are less noticeable than traditional metal braces.

  • Least visible

    Clarity Braces

    Clarity braces are hard and clear ceramic braces that are both strong and significantly less visible than regular braces. People of all ages choose clarity braces in Dublin and Tracy, CA, for their aesthetic appeal and the confidence it gives them to smile, even before their treatment is completed.

Living with braces — what actually changes

The first few days feel new: teeth are tender, and your tongue finds every bracket. That settles within a week, and from then on braces slot into ordinary life. What matters most is keeping teeth spotless while the brackets are on, so the smile underneath is as healthy as it is straight.

  • Brush after every meal, angling the brush above and below each bracket
  • Floss daily with threaders or a water flosser
  • Skip hard, sticky and chewy foods — ice, caramel, hard bread crusts
  • Cut apples, carrots and corn off the cob into smaller pieces
  • Wear a mouthguard for contact sport
  • Use orthodontic wax on any bracket that rubs while you settle in

Braces questions we answer every week

How long will I need to wear braces?

Most patients finish in 18 to 24 months. Mild crowding or spacing can be done sooner; complex bite corrections take longer. Dr. Gonzales gives you an estimated range at your first assessment, based on your own scan rather than an average.

Do braces hurt?

Bonding the brackets is painless. Teeth usually feel tender for three to five days afterwards and for a day or so after each adjustment. Soft food and an over-the-counter pain reliever handle it, and orthodontic wax covers anything that rubs.

What is the best age for braces?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first check-up by age seven, when we can see how the adult teeth and jaws are developing. Full treatment usually begins between 10 and 14 — but healthy teeth and gums matter far more than age, and we treat adults every week.

Should I choose ceramic braces or metal braces?

Ceramic and Clarity brackets are far less noticeable, which is why most adults prefer them. Metal brackets are the strongest and best suited to complex movements — and teens often like choosing new elastic colours. Both straighten teeth to the same standard, so the decision usually comes down to appearance and the mechanics your case needs.

How much do braces cost in Dublin and Tracy?

Cost depends on how much movement your case needs and how long treatment runs. We quote the full fee in writing at your complimentary assessment, offer interest-free monthly plans, and file your orthodontic insurance benefit for you.

What happens if a bracket comes loose?

Call the office. A loose bracket is rarely urgent, but leaving it slows treatment, so we fit you in quickly — often the same day. If a wire is poking, cover the end with wax until we see you.

Will I need a retainer afterwards?

Yes. Teeth drift for life, so retainers are what keep the result. We fit custom retainers on the day your braces come off and back them with the Gonzales Lifetime Smile Guarantee.
